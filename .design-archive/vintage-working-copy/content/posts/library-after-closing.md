There is a different library hidden inside every library. It appears after the practical questions have gone home: *Where is the printer? When do you close? Do you have this book?*

What remains is a room full of postponed conversations.

I like wandering without a title in mind. Classification becomes a suggestion rather than a command. One shelf offers an argument, the next a field guide, the next a book that has not been checked out since 1987 and seems relieved to see someone.

This is sample writing for the first version of The Monoblogue. It exists to test the reading layout and should be replaced when the real shelves arrive.

## A small rule for wandering

Leave with one thing you meant to find and one thing you did not.
