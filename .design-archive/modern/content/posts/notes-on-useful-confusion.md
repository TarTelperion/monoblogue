Confusion is usually treated as a hallway: an unpleasant space between knowing one thing and knowing another. But sometimes the hallway contains the interesting furniture.

When I understand a subject too quickly, I often inherit somebody else's boundaries along with their explanation. Staying confused for a little longer makes room for worse questions, which are frequently the ones I actually needed.
