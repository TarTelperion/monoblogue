Rules are useful until they begin pretending to be the language.

I learned *por* and *para* first as two columns of reasons. That worked for exercises and failed in conversation. The distinction became more durable when I began collecting complete sentences instead of exceptions.

This sample entry demonstrates the language archive and will eventually be replaced with a real note.
