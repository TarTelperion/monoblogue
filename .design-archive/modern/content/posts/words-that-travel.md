Languages borrow from one another with the confidence of neighbors who have stopped keeping track of whose ladder is whose.

A borrowed word rarely arrives empty. It carries weather, habits, old jokes, and assumptions about what deserves a name. Learning those histories is a way of noticing that a dictionary is not a vault. It is a train station.

This sample post demonstrates that a Library entry can belong to more than one language.
